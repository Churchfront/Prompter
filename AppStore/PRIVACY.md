# CuePrompter Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

CuePrompter is a teleprompter app made by Churchfront LLC. This policy explains
what the app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect
any of it.

## What we collect

Nothing. CuePrompter has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
servers of its own. Churchfront does not receive your scripts, your voice or
any information about how you use the app.

## Your scripts and settings

Scripts you write, paste or import, and your reading preferences, are saved on
your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Microphone and speech recognition

Voice Follow uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to match what
you say to your script. Audio is processed live and is never recorded or saved
by CuePrompter.

Where your device supports it, recognition happens entirely on the device.
Where it does not, iOS may send audio to Apple's speech recognition service to
be transcribed, under Apple's own privacy policy. Timed Scroll never uses the
microphone.

## Phone remote and second screens

If you turn on the phone remote, CuePrompter serves a web page to other devices
on your local Wi-Fi network so they can control or show the prompter. These
pages stay on your local network and require no sign-in, so anyone on the same
network who has the address can open them. The feature is off until you turn
it on.

## Notion import

If you choose to import scripts from Notion, the integration token you enter is
stored in your device's Keychain and sent only to Notion (api.notion.com) to
read the pages you shared with it. CuePrompter never writes to Notion.
Notion's own privacy policy applies to that connection.

## Children

CuePrompter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
