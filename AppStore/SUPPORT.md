# CuePrompter Support

CuePrompter is a teleprompter for iPhone and iPad that follows your voice.
Questions or problems: email matt@churchfront.com and we will reply.

## Getting started

1. Open the sample script, or tap New Script and paste your own.
2. Tap Start, then Play, and read out loud. The script moves with you.
3. Prefer a steady scroll? Choose Timed and set the speed in words per minute.

## Common questions

**The script does not move when I read.**
Voice Follow needs the microphone and speech recognition. Go to Settings,
CuePrompter, and turn both on. If you denied them, the app shows a shortcut to
Settings, or offers Timed Scroll instead.

**How do I use it with beam-splitter glass?**
Tap the mirror button at the top of the prompter. Only the script flips; the
controls stay readable.

**Can I run it from my phone?**
Yes. In the library, tap the phone icon and turn on the phone remote. Scan the
QR code with your phone to control the session, and open the display address on
an iPad or any screen with a browser to show the prompter there. All devices
must be on the same Wi-Fi network, and CuePrompter must be allowed Local Network
access.

**Do you see my scripts?**
No. Scripts stay on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

**Can I bring in scripts from Notion?**
Yes. In the library, choose Import, then From Notion, and follow the one-time
setup on screen.

## Keyboard shortcuts (iPad with a keyboard or pedal)

Space, Return or B plays and pauses. Up and down arrows, or Page Up and Page
Down (what most presentation pedals send), jump by sentence. Left and right
arrows change the Timed Scroll speed. M mirrors the script. Command-R restarts.
